## Title

How Netflix and Riot Games Choose Their Software Development Processes

## Description

How do software teams choose a development process when their systems and release demands are so different?

This project compares Netflix's cloud-native streaming platform with Riot Games' live-service multiplayer game, League of Legends. It looks at requirements specification, design and implementation, verification and validation, and continuous evolution—alongside approaches such as Waterfall, incremental development, integration and configuration, and Agile.

Netflix's microservices-driven DevOps supports continuous deployments, feature flags, A/B telemetry, and resilience practices such as Chaos Engineering. Riot Games uses a hybrid Agile-incremental approach, with synchronized global patches, public beta playtesting, and multi-tier validation.

The key takeaway: there is no universally best software process. Effective choices depend on architecture, team organization, operational risk, and release frequency.
