What software development process works best? It depends on what you're building.

For my project, **“Comparing Software Process Models in Cloud-Native and Live-Service Architectures,”** I explored how Netflix and Riot Games make different process choices to meet very different architectural and operational needs.

Netflix's cloud-native streaming platform relies on microservices-driven DevOps, continuous deployments, feature flags, A/B telemetry, and resilience practices such as Chaos Engineering. Riot Games' League of Legends uses a hybrid Agile-incremental framework shaped by synchronized global patches, public beta playtesting, and multi-tier verification.

The comparison considers requirements specification, design and implementation, verification and validation, and continuous evolution. The central takeaway is that no single process model fits every system: architecture, team topology, operational risk, and release frequency all matter.

This project was completed for the Software Process Models & Comparative Systems Engineering track at Concordia University Chicago, Fall 2026.
